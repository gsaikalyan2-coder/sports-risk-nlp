04

Text ↔ physiology concordance

strongest scientifically

Does what the athlete says agree with what their body shows? Two channels on one timeline. Where they diverge — calm words over high arousal — the band fills. That pattern has a name in the literature (repressive coping) and it is exactly the athlete a text-only system misses.
